MT5 Router uses mitmproxy_rs 0.12.11 and WinDivert from mitmproxy_windows 0.12.11.
The Windows redirector is modified for native executable/port/IP selection.
Its LGPL-3.0-or-later source and exact upstream archive are in native-source.zip.
Full LGPL/GPL texts are included in WinDivert.txt.
